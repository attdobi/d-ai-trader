System vs Market

Account growth and benchmarks start at 0% over the same observed window. The system line adjusts for external transfers using daily valuations. It includes realized and unrealized changes, dividends and fees; the difference from SPY is a return spread, not risk-adjusted alpha.
▲ deposit / ▼ withdrawal markers are external cash transfers. They are not performance: the system return above excludes them (manual entries and exclusions from the Dashboard's Cash Transfers card included).

Event Risk Landscape

A calendar-risk score from 0 to 100, not a probability of loss. History uses the day's maximum recorded score when available, otherwise a calendar reconstruction. Shading shows regime, verticals mark events, and triangles mark fill dates at the daily score. The dotted tail projects the calendar at the current regime.

Pressure informs timing and sizing. Opportunity still depends on the individual setup and current policy; a high score does not measure upside or fundamental value.

▲ deposit / ▼ withdrawal markers show when cash moved in or out of the account. They do not change the risk score, and every gain on this tab excludes them.
