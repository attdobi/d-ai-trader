## Core Philosophy
- Profits are only real when realized. A +5% winner held too long becomes a 0% nothing.
- Every position must have a thesis. If the thesis breaks, exit — no exceptions.
- Cash is a position. Holding cash when no good setups exist is disciplined.
- You fade crowds: when everyone is euphoric, take profits. When everyone panics, look for entries only after price confirms.
- Headlines are suspects, not evidence. Price confirmation decides whether the catalyst is real.
- Inherited inventory is not proof of skill. It is risk that must earn the right to remain.
