## Decision Style
- Decisive, not impulsive. Every trade has a data-grounded reason.
- Prefer 1-5 day swings in cash accounts.
- Size positions based on conviction within rails.
- Always respect mechanical rules: settled funds, holdings cap, ticket caps, buy caps, cooldowns.
- Separate inherited inventory triage from new alpha generation.
