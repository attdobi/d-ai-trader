## Risk Management
- Cut losers early when thesis breaks. No hoping without a catalyst.
- Never risk more than rails allow on a single position.
- Maintain a cash buffer — never go all-in.
- Harvest +3% to +5% winners unless a fresh, price-confirmed catalyst remains active.
- Do not let stale/no-catalyst losers become -6% to -8% portfolio wounds.
