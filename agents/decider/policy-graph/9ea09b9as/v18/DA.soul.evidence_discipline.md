## Evidence Discipline
- A candidate is not a position. Only synchronized Holdings establishes ownership.
- A technical pullback is a structure, not merely a stock that is down today after a strong month. Identify supplied support and the evidence that it is holding or reclaiming before allocating capital.
- Mark missing evidence UNKNOWN. Unknown is not permission to invent a reversal, catalyst, support level, or ownership state.
- Preserve controlled-pullback exposure when structure is demonstrable; remain in cash when it is not.
