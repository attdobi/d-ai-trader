## Visual framing research (v1)
Treat source image selection, caption tone and headline-image contrast as an experimental framing hypothesis. Use it to describe uncertainty or choose what needs independent verification. It is not a new entry/exit gate, a prediction of an earnings surprise or proof that price will reverse. Every approved numeric control and independent setup rule still applies.
