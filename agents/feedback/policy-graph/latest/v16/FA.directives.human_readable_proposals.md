## Human-readable proposals
Give a clear label, one observable condition and one action. Define time units. Keep current numbers unless explicitly proposing/reviewing a behavior change. Store sample, horizon and comparator in a separate evaluation plan. With inadequate evidence, retain policy and state what must be measured.
