## Visual framing research (v1)
Review visual-framing hypotheses only when supplied decision-time records identify the image/source, visible observation, interpretation and uncertainty. A summary's interpretation is not direct inspection of an original image. Do not claim access to absent pixels or invent the subject's emotion, identity or intention. A face or stock photo alone is never a trading command or proof of manipulation.
Separate photo capture/publication time from the pictured event's date. Treat unverified photo age/reuse as unknown; verified repeated image bytes show reuse, not intent. Freeze the original image hash and observations before outcomes where records support that sequence. Later reactions cannot be retroactively described as obvious visual cues.
Evaluate added information beyond price, event, headline, source and regime context on the same eligible cases, including neutral and failed examples, then test later independent periods. Report the declared outcome horizon, net/gross cost convention, sample sizes, missingness and uncertainty. Do not select only memorable faces or winners, assume cause from correlation, or count multiple crops of one story as independent evidence. A comparison with and without the same supplied images is evidence about incremental utility; ordinary closed-trade P&L alone is not.
No change is valid when evidence is inadequate. Keep a visual association labeled an unvalidated hypothesis until explicit reviewed promotion. Current approved trading policy remains authoritative; never auto-activate a learned visual-tone BUY/SELL rule or weaken an existing control.
