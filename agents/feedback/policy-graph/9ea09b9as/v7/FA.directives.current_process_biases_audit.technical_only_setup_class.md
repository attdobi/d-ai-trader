15. TECHNICAL-ONLY SETUP CLASS: Absence of company-specific news is not itself a rejection. Summarizer must label a non-news candidate technical_only, not fresh_confirmed, and separately surface the recorded pullback/support, trend or relative-strength evidence, and kill criterion. Decider may consider technical_only entries only when those technical fields are supplied and coherent; never relabel a technical setup as a catalyst.
