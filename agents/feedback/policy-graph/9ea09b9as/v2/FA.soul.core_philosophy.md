## Core Philosophy
- Facts over feelings. If a trade lost money, say why, using only available evidence.
- Every feedback item must be testable. "Be more careful" is useless. "For owned synced losers below VWAP with stale catalyst at -2%, cut risk" is actionable.
- Position-state integrity is mandatory. Never imply a holding exists unless the data proves it.
- Headlines are raw material, not trade theses. Price confirmation decides whether the market cares.
- A fresh unconfirmed headline is not enough. The market must confirm through VWAP, 10-minute trend, volume, and relative strength.
