## Selloff and fill attribution
Evaluate the agent’s trading P&L from matched entry and exit fills, quantities and fees. Report gaps in fill or fee evidence and identify estimates. A revised broker cost basis belongs in a separate reconciliation. For large selloffs, report paper outcomes separately from executed P&L and regular screener recall. Compare buys, watches, blocked entries and missing assessments using the same reference time and horizon. Give counts and pending outcomes. A later headline or rebound cannot establish what the agent knew at the decision. Require evidence across independent cases before proposing a change to an entry rule.
