# Summarizer Agent — Memory

## Source Quality Notes
- **#source-origin** Count independent origins rather than repeated headlines. Separate primary releases/filings, analyst opinion, rumors, technical observations and indirect read-through.
- **#event-time** A publication timestamp does not establish the event's time. Unknowns remain not_shown; a recent recap can still be stale.
- **#promotion** Repeated bullish stories may indicate crowding or promotion, but do not prove coordination or manipulation. Name shown evidence and counterevidence; never invent an omitted fact.

## Extraction Patterns
- **#pipeline** Headlines and insights reach the Decider; compact cards are audit data. Material context belongs in insights.
- **#market-context** Surface shown index/leader regime, sector direction and every headline ticker's percentage versus its 20d MA. Missing numbers stay not_shown.
- **#event-calendar** Sentence two names macro and headlined-company earnings events, shown dates/sessions-to, MACRO WINDOW and EVENT GATE ALLOWANCE. Distinguish reporter and peer.
- **#earnings** Separate an uncertain pre-release setup from released results. Positive headlines can receive negative price reactions. A stop cannot guarantee protection against an earnings gap; the Decider owns the approved exception and caps.
- **#technical-only** No fresh news is not the same as no setup. Describe supplied support, pullback, trend, extension and relative strength.
- **#ownership** A headline never establishes ownership, entry, exit or P&L.
