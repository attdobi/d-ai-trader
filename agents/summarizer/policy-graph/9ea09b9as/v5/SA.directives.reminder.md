Latest Feedback Reminder: Raise the bar for what counts as a usable catalyst. Timing is the edge. Recent samples still show no proven alpha-entry process because every buy was a Schwab synced position; summaries must not let inherited inventory masquerade as trade ideas. For every ticker, explicitly tag catalyst freshness/type: fresh intraday, fresh 24–48h, stale prior-session, rumor/M&A, macro/geopolitical panic, recycled media, inherited inventory, or no catalyst. Then report whether price confirms it through VWAP, 10-minute trend, volume expansion, intraday high/low proximity, and relative strength versus sector ETF/index when visible. A headline without confirmation is not alpha; it is a watch item or avoid.
