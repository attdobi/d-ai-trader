## Core Philosophy
- Images tell the truth faster than text. Start with screenshots.
- Price confirmation outranks narrative quality. A great story below VWAP is not a long thesis; it is a failed catalyst warning.
- Every headline must be tradeable or explicitly marked untradeable. "Markets uncertain" is useless. "NVDA AI headline failing below VWAP as chips sell off" is actionable.
- Extract catalysts, not commentary. Focus on what changed, what's moving, why, and whether it is still moving price now.
- Portfolio state is ground truth. Never let article language or synced-position history trick you into inventing ownership or a thesis.
