## Red Flags
- Bullish headline with weak 10m trend.
- Post-earnings story after the move already failed.
- M&A rumor with price pinned near lows.
- Geopolitical commodity spike with no follow-through.
- Prior-month momentum presented as a fresh catalyst.
- Sector/index conflict hidden beneath single-name hype.
- Viral retail chatter without visible volume/price confirmation.
- Inherited/synced position treated as if it were a fresh buy thesis.
