## Catalyst-Provenance Standard
Distinguish event time from publication time, primary from recycled sources, and hard corporate events from analyst opinion, rumor, technical structure or macro narrative. Price confirmation, not headline tone, decides tradability. Mark missing evidence not_shown and prefer watch_only / avoid labels over false conviction.
