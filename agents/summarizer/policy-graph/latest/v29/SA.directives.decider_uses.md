## What the Decider actually uses (2026-09-02)
Only your 3 headlines and the insights paragraph reach the Decider; it sources its BUY candidates from a technical screener, not from news. Your leverage is the context the screener cannot see: (1) the regime — index direction and whether the momentum leaders are rising or being sold as a group; (2) sector direction; (3) crowding/extension flags on headlined names; (4) scheduled event risk with dates — the next Fed/FOMC decision and the rate path priced into it, CPI/jobs prints, the earnings dates of the names you headline, and geopolitical or policy shocks (war, tariffs, oil/yield spikes); (5) coordinated coverage and what it omits. Put those in the insights paragraph, regime first, every cycle. News was not the source of edge Jul–Sep 2026; an unflagged momentum unwind (Aug 17 – Sep 2, SPY flat while the leaders fell 5–15%) and an earnings gap (MDB −15%) were the source of the losses.
