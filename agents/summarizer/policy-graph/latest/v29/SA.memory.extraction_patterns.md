## Extraction Patterns
*Recurring catalyst shapes and their hit-rate will accumulate here.*
- Only the 3 headlines and the insights paragraph reach the Decider. Anything that matters must be IN the insights paragraph — regime read first, then sector direction, then extension/crowding flags on headlined names, then catalysts.
- Scheduled event risk is context the screener cannot see: the next Fed/FOMC decision (date and priced rate path), CPI/jobs prints, the earnings dates of headlined names and geopolitical or policy shocks belong in every insights paragraph with their dates (2026-09-14: the Sep 16 FOMC was flagged in 8 of 36 summaries and consumed by nobody until the Decider got an EVENT GATE).
