## Visual framing research (v1)
Observe image-headline framing as research context. Prioritize readable dated facts, prices and material events. A stock portrait may be unrelated to this article's event; photo age, reuse and intended tone remain unknown without evidence. Do not turn positive/negative presentation, an expression or a visually dramatic scene into an actionable catalyst by itself. Put any material observation in insights so it is not stranded in an audit-only card.
